Critical Findings 05 / 15

Six Issues Requiring Immediate Attention

Critical · W1

No Unified Customer Identity

Customers create separate accounts for each division. No cross-division intelligence; fragmented experience.

Critical · W10

No Cross-Division Bundling

Vehicle + insurance + financing bundles not offered. Missed revenue; competitors capture bundled needs.

Critical · V2

Missing Security Headers

No HSTS, CSP, or X-Frame-Options. Elevated risk of clickjacking and XSS attacks.

Critical · V7

Payment Crediting Delay

Customer reviews cite 5-day delay for account credit via TDC's own payment site. Trust erosion.

High · W4

No Online Loan Application

Financial Services requires branch visits or calls. High friction; excludes after-hours applicants.

High · W5

No Instant Insurance Quote

Quotes answered manually within 24 hours. Competitors offer instant quotes online.

Solution Architecture 08 / 15

Five-Layer Modular Architecture

1
Presentation Layer
React/TypeScript + Tailwind. Customer Portal, Staff Portal, Executive Dashboard. Progressive Web App for mobile.
2
API Gateway
Authentication, rate limiting, routing, monitoring, logging. Single entry point for all client requests.
3
Business Logic
Domain modules per division. Shared services: Identity, Payments, Notifications, Documents.
4
Data Layer
PostgreSQL (primary), Redis (cache), S3 (documents), Elasticsearch (search).
5
Integration Layer
QuickBooks Online, Payment Gateways, SMS/Email, Customs, Credit Bureau. API-first design.
