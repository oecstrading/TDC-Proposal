Executive Summary

TDC's Digital Estate at a Crossroads

Part 2 · Methodology & Design

Systems Analysis Approach

The structured methodology used to move from current-state assessment to solution design — including stakeholder mapping, requirements elicitation, gap analysis, and architectural decisions.

🔍

Phase 1 · Discovery

Organisational reconnaissance, division research, and current-state assessment. Review of financial reports, public commentary, and digital touchpoints.

👥

Phase 2 · Stakeholder Mapping

Identification of Executive, Division Managers, Finance, IT, Front-Line Staff, Customers, and Regulators with their concerns and success metrics.

📋

Phase 3 · Process Inventory

Cataloguing core processes across Retail, Automotive, Financial Services, Insurance, Shipping, and Real Estate — from enquiry to after-sales.

📐

Phase 4 · Requirements Elicitation

Functional, non-functional, and integration requirements derived from process inventory and stakeholder needs.

📊

Phase 5 · Gap Analysis

Comparison of current-state capabilities against desired-state across 10 domains — identifying high, medium, and low gaps.

🏗️

Phase 6 · Solution Design

Modular monolith with API-first design. Technology stack selection, security architecture, and integration patterns.

🎨

Phase 7 · Prototyping

Working HTML prototypes that make requirements tangible and enable stakeholder feedback in hours rather than weeks.

🚀

Phase 8 · Validation & Delivery

Stakeholder review, phased implementation planning, and mobilisation for delivery.

Single Customer View

One identity, one profile, all divisions. Every interaction enriches a unified customer record.

Progressive Disclosure

Show what's needed, reveal complexity on demand. Simple for casual users, powerful for power users.

Mobile-First

Most Caribbean customers access via mobile. Every screen designed for small viewports first.

Offline-Capable

Critical for Nevis and rural areas. Progressive Web App with service worker caching.

Audit-Ready

Every transaction traceable. Immutable audit logs. Compliance built-in, not bolted-on.

Composable

Modules can be deployed independently. Start with one division, expand without rework.

Secure by Design

Zero-trust, least-privilege, encrypted at rest and in transit. Security is not an afterthought.

API-First

Every capability exposed as an API. Enables partners, mobile apps, and future innovation.
