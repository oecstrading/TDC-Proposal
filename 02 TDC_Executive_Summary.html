TDC Group · Digital Transformation Proposal Executive Summary

Executive Overview

TDC Group of Companies is one of the most diversified business groups in the Eastern Caribbean — nine divisions, 500+ employees, and EC$137.5M in annual revenue. Yet the Group's digital estate remains at an early stage of maturity. This proposal presents an evidence-based assessment, a systems design, and a phased 18-month roadmap to transform TDC into a digitally integrated enterprise.

"TDC's divisions are strong individually, but its digital presence is fragmented. Every day without a unified platform is a day of lost revenue, redundant cost, and unaddressed customer friction."

What We Found

An independent review of TDC's public digital properties — tdcgrouplimited.com, shoptdcgroup.com, tdcairbox.com, and tdcgroupinsurance.com — identified 23 weaknesses, 8 vulnerabilities, and 14 strategic gaps. Only 2 of 9 divisions offer any online transactional capability. There is no unified customer identity, no cross-division bundling, and no shared back-office integration. Customer reviews reference payment crediting delays. Site performance indicators show load times above 3 seconds.

Critically, the Group's digital maturity scores 2.8 out of 10. Comparable regional conglomerates with unified platforms operate at 6–7. This gap represents both risk and opportunity.

The Opportunity

A unified platform will deliver three categories of value:

Revenue growth. Cross-division selling — vehicle + insurance + financing; home improvement + loan + shipping — can lift Group revenue by an estimated 5–8% annually. Improved digital conversion and new digital-only customers add further upside.

Cost reduction. Elimination of duplicate data entry, automated inventory reordering, reduced paper processing, and improved staff productivity can deliver EC$2.8M–$4.0M in annual savings.

Strategic positioning. Real-time Group analytics, omnichannel customer experience, and a scalable platform position TDC as the digital leader in the Eastern Caribbean — protecting market share and enabling future expansion.

Top 6 Critical Findings

Critical · W1

No Unified Customer Identity

Customers create separate accounts for shop, Airbox, and insurance. Fragmented experience; no cross-division intelligence.

Critical · W10

No Cross-Division Bundling

Vehicle + insurance + financing bundles are not offered. Missed revenue; competitors capture bundled needs.

Critical · V2

Missing Security Headers

No HSTS, CSP, or X-Frame-Options. Increased risk of clickjacking, XSS, and MITM attacks.

Critical · V7

Payment Crediting Delay

Customer reviews cite 5-day delay for account credit via TDC's own payment site. Trust erosion risk.

High · W4

No Online Loan Application

Financial Services requires branch visits or phone calls. High friction; excludes after-hours applicants.

High · W5

No Instant Insurance Quote

Quote requests answered manually within 24 hours. Competitors offer instant quotes.
